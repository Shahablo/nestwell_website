What happens next

  1. We reply within a few business days from hello@hellonestwell.com, with two or three times for a call.
  2. Thirty minutes, four questions. Who owns the postpartum gap today; which tools you have and what remains unresolved; who reads a same-day note on a Friday afternoon; who would pay, from which budget.
  3. A scope sheet, if it makes sense. A fixed pilot fee or a per-enrolled-episode price, with support limits written down. Clinical services stay with the practice.
  4. Or an honest “not yet”. If you already have this covered, or the timing is wrong, we would rather hear it in the call than after a pilot cycle.
